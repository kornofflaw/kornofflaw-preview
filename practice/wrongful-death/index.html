Practice Area

California Wrongful Death Litigation

Civil claims after a preventable death — including matters that arise from violent or criminal conduct when a civil remedy is available. Plaintiff or defense, depending on the matter.

Who this is for

  • Families seeking accountability after a loss
  • Parties and insurers facing a wrongful death claim
  • Counsel needing complex-civil support in California state or federal court

What “complex” means here

Multiple defendants, coverage fights, difficult liability facts, or parallel proceedings. Not a volume personal-injury pipeline.

Civil — not criminal

Criminal labels stay off this site. When death follows alleged criminal conduct, the civil wrongful death claim is a separate path with its own proof, damages, and defenses.
